## Comparative assessment and decision value

**Case A:** All three correctly separated proven in-flight/ingress races from newly mandatory OS descendant death, native cgroups and fleet/protocol-drain certification. An authoritative activity fence plus exact CAS and honest refusal of unknown active jobs can satisfy the bounded ledger correction without asserting process death. However, if the user-visible contract explicitly requires a positive correction for agents with unresolved detached jobs, a conservative permanent refusal could miss an availability requirement; Lead must clarify that semantic boundary before calling such states supported. None of the six proposed dropping genuine activity safety.

**Case B:** All three correctly classified the same-task/same-owner/new-assignment stale session as a must-fix original acceptance failure, not overengineering. The minimal safeguard is caller/session-bound immutable assignment identity validated transactionally, plus a test that S1/gen1 cannot Complete/Checkpoint/Yield S2/gen2 even with omitted versions, while S2 can proceed and idempotent retries return recorded outcomes. A broader revocation framework is not needed merely to provide this guard.

**Role comparison:** In this two-case pilot, the Critical Senior Dev added no distinct decision beyond the pragmatic Lead. The Standard Reviewer uniquely stressed individual deterministic A-race regressions and B recovery rebinding; the pragmatic Lead stressed autonomous separation of product scope and stable replay. None changed the binary accept/reject classification. Every role got 9/10, with a methodological independence penalty; this is a ceiling effect on **two selected, heavily directed cases**, not an estimated success rate. Lead-alone remains the default. A selective additional consultant may be useful for an unusually material, genuinely ambiguous Challenge, but its cost must be demonstrated against a less directed baseline before a mandatory process is justified.

**Overhead:** Exactly six separate ChatGPT conversations were launched by Lead; their raw prompts/outputs were later retrieved by Worker 2. Extra model calls and manual Send/verification are real coordination overhead. Input/output tokens, launch-to-finish times, comparable API costs, model IDs and total human/agent coordination time were not exposed. Do not transform response word counts or observation timestamps into token/latency estimates. No new consultations were launched by Worker 2.
