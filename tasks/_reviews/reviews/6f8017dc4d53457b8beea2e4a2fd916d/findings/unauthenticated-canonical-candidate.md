# P0: Candidate provenance is shape-checked, not authenticated to the frozen project ledger

**Frozen source:** `5e37133d5f1b7e7c6e13c890b662caf14eafa9c8` (parent `a422338e64b82026d25b28e6b63287f945b8bb73`). **Original task:** `scheduler-native-account-reservation-cas-20261121`, acceptance requiring exact Worker task/attempt/generation/source lease or Reviewer immutable review/version/source/ref **plus authenticated Reviewer claim**, and fail-closed missing/forged project proofs.

**Observed source:** `Client/Modules/NativeSchedulerAccountReservation.fs:114-145` checks roster membership and the *syntax* of the supplied candidate fields. It neither obtains an authenticated project Work/assignment/review snapshot nor compares the purported task/attempt/assignment-generation/source-lease or ReviewId/version/claim-operation/source/head to a canonical project record. `reserveVerifiedDisposable` lines 223-269 relies entirely on `candidateValid` before writing an immutable account receipt. `Client.Tests/NativeSchedulerRosterPolicyTests.fs:621-647` creates random AttemptId, synthetic `SourceRepositoryId = "aaaa..."`, `SourceLeaseSha256 = "bbbb..."`, and a random Reviewer ClaimOperationId/ReviewId; the happy-path tests then successfully reserve them. Those projects have no corresponding canonical task/claim; their signed roster establishes membership, **not** that the candidate was actually issued by the project ledger.

**Impact and boundary:** This is not a live exploit: public `reserve` and `read` are deliberately disabled and the writable entrypoint is disposable-test-scoped. Nevertheless, the newly added account reservation path does **not yet establish the candidate authority promised by the task**. A future caller presenting a syntactically plausible but unauthenticated candidate can consume capacity or misbind a supposed review/session if this path is promoted without a source-bound verifier. Request-hashed receipts preserve the wrong candidate faithfully; they do not authenticate its origin.

**Reproduction/negative control:** In the existing disposable fixture, keep the signed roster and valid coverage but supply a fresh Worker candidate with a random non-existent TaskId/AttemptId and 64-hex source-lease digest, or a Reviewer with a random unclaimed ReviewId/version/ClaimOperationId. The fixture already does this in lines 621-647 and the successful tests at 662-686, 721-742 demonstrate acceptance. There is no rejection case for a forged but well-formed candidate.

**Proportionate remedy:** Preserve the default-off production boundary. Before claiming native candidate authority, require a verifier of the *same authenticated frozen project head* that derives/cross-checks exact candidate identity, issued Worker source lease or independently recorded immutable Reviewer claim; fail closed on absent/mismatched evidence. Add explicit negatives for fake IDs, rewritten lease/head/ref/claim and stale project-head TOCTOU. The verifier need not authorize browser sends. This finding is about existing original task requirements, not a demand to enable production admission or introduce a new host root.
