# Required real-caller failure cases are absent

The task explicitly requires coverage of the actual caller with a controllable bridge failing before and after send, as well as unknown occupancy and no replay. At frozen commit 78a2ffb6f044b2f30a79a57f5d348c5e3c4fa376, `ActualCaller.fs` exercises HOLD and a successful openTab/executeJs response only. `Tests.fs` `failureAfterBeginRetainsOccupancyAndNeverReplays` throws synchronously from a synthetic callback; it never calls `receiveCommand` or causes the bridge to fail. Thus it does not test how the real asynchronous caller/bridge reports pre-send rejection or post-send ambiguity, or whether the reservation remains occupied across those outcomes. The original task's distinguishing failure cases are unverified, despite the positive test passing the real caller through a disposable reservation.

Candidate remedy: exercise the composed real caller against the controllable bridge for a definite failure before the external send and a lost/error outcome after the bridge receives the send, assert durable occupancy and no replay (and release only with genuine original-bound exclusion). Preserve production HOLD and fixture-evidence boundaries. This finding is scoped to the original acceptance requirement; it does not mandate a new production admission path.
