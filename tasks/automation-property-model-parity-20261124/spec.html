Global invariants

  • No double ownership, duplicate effective dispatch or release; stale generations cannot mutate successor ownership.
  • Capacity conserved across account/project reservation and effect lifecycles; uncertain effects remain occupied until supported reconciliation. No release from mere tab closure or absent telemetry.
  • Blocked work cannot dispatch; satisfied conditions cannot lose wakeups. Under bounded fair deterministic progress and available capacity, continuously eligible work eventually receives a dispatch opportunity; distinguish safety from conditional liveness.
  • Health recovery cannot violate Challenge dependency, original launch lineage, STOP or Scheduler fences; concurrent recovery and scheduling preserve exactly one durable successful transition where required.
  • Canonical history, indices, checkpoints and known-versus-unknown evidence remain consistent across retries, partial failure, restart and mixed-version receipts. No invented effect or replay.
