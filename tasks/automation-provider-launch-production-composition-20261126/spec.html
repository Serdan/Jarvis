Operator architectural correction — provider independence and leases, 2026-11-27

Execution identity/authority is a Jarvis concern, already addressed in the Jarvis 2.0 design by Jarvis-issued leases injected into the prompt. The execution presents its lease when calling Jarvis; Jarvis validates scope, current ownership and validity against canonical authority. Provider adapters deliver the supplied prompt and report transport observations. They must not issue, establish, attest or own execution identity, role authority or authorization. This supersedes any preceding requirement to obtain a provider-specific authenticated execution binding as an architectural prerequisite. Account authentication and execution authorization remain separate; use existing authentication plus the canonical lease contract, not a new provider signing mechanism. Tool visibility/provisioning is separate from authorization; enforce authorization independently at Jarvis, not from provider labels or selected MCP inventories. Do not treat the reserved deny-only provider namespace as a required future architecture. Keep the existing deny behavior until a reviewed replacement; no auth bypass, credential leakage, live provider activation, or source changes authorized by this clarification. Production provider composition remains deferred/unassigned; reassess its scope in 2.0 rather than adding identity infrastructure to Automation 1.0. Preserve original effect fencing and canonical generation/lease semantics.
