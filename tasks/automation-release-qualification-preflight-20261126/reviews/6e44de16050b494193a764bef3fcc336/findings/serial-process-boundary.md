## Defect: leader exit does not establish serial stage termination
Frozen SHA 6e44de16050bad9413a764bef3fcc3363a0310dd, qualify.py run_stage. Original task explicitly requires serial heavy suites, bounded deadlines, durable exit/hash evidence. The runner waits only for Popen leader and kills process group only on timeout; after normal leader exit it hashes stage files and marks exited, then may start next stage. Independent bounded disposable Python fixture spawned a same-group child that slept 0.8s and wrote late.txt in the stage after the parent exited. run_stage returned True, terminal state exited; late.txt absent at receipt but present 1.2s later, not in hashed manifest. No production process was used. This shows a child can overlap later stages or change evidence after receipt, especially on abnormal test-host/build-tool exits. Existing MSBuild no-node-reuse flags mitigate but do not establish process-tree quiescence. Candidate remedy: enforce a bounded process-group/container completion policy on every exit, fail/stop on uncertain surviving descendants, and test late writers; do not claim arbitrary escaped-descendant containment without an appropriate OS isolation mechanism. Exact remedy subject to independent adjudication.
