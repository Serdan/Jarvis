Simplify agent execution state to Idle and Working

In the current Jarvis runtime, expose two agent execution states: Idle and Working. The state describes whether the agent is executing a turn. Keep assignments, runnable work, review claims, checkpoints, job dependencies and blockers in their respective records. Replace legacy agent statuses such as Ready, Parked and NeedsWork at the user-facing and scheduling boundaries. Preserve the behavior of existing ledger data through a compatible projection or migration appropriate to the existing persistence model.

When Jarvis accepts a tool call attributed to an agent's own ID, transition that agent from Idle to Working regardless of assignment or claim and record activity. Calls attributed to an observer must not change the observed agent. A successful terminal turn operation transitions Working to Idle without an activation during the same call. If the turn yields on a running job, preserve the work's durable continuation and dependency. Health recovery returns a verified stale Working session to Idle and preserves its continuation. Handle concurrent activation, terminal calls and stale recovery explicitly.

Scheduler eligibility comes from queue demand, role and runnable work plus Idle state. A launch in flight is tracked by the existing launch record until attributed activity or reconciliation, outside the agent state. Preserve duplicate-send and unknown-outcome fencing, caps based on Working agents, the per-project enable switch, explicit manual launch and existing review independence. Coordinate with automation-role-aware-reviewer-dispatch. This is a follow-up to the current rollout and does not gate it or replace the separate Work-native persistent-agent retirement effort.

Verify registered assigned and taskless calls both activate, observation leaves its target unchanged, explicit end and stale recovery return to Idle, job-blocked work remains waiting while its agent is Idle, and scheduling does not repeat a launch in flight. Verify existing ledger records and TUI idle visibility, including Interpreter without a standing assignment. Exercise the registered tool and scheduler routes in disposable fixtures and obtain independent review.
