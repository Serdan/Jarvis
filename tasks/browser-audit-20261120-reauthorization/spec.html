[P1][Browser audit] Make browser reauthorization explicit and recoverable for unattended schedules

Finding

A registered window moved to needsReauthorization after a browser-session change. All recurring launches failed WindowUnavailable:needsReauthorization while remaining pending, and manual reauthorization later restored readiness.

Required work

Trace extension browser-session epoch, registration restore and scheduler failure handling. Define an explicit authorization/recovery state: never silently trust a previous browser authorization, but expose the required operator action, pause or back off launches until registration is valid, and automatically resume only schedules that remain authorized and enabled. Avoid repeated opaque failure attempts and ensure the selected window registration/generation is respected. Keep the authorization boundary; do not add auto-registration or privilege bypass.

Acceptance criteria

  • Browser restart, extension-worker restart, session-epoch mismatch, manual reauthorization, window revocation and unavailable device are independently tested.
  • The scheduler clearly distinguishes registration-required from transient browser failures and avoids futile repeated launch attempts while unauthorized.
  • Authorized recurring work resumes at a future occurrence without replaying uncertain or already-submitted prompts.
  • One clean deployed-extension acceptance run demonstrates the end-to-end transition and records the extension/client versions.

Evidence and starting points

Client/Modules/BrowserBridge.fs; Client/Modules/BrowserSchedules.fs; browser-extension/background.js; main window generation 4 needsReauthorization, subsequently generation 5 ready.

Boundaries

Do not launch agents, change running schedules, deploy, or perform live prompt submissions as a side effect of this task's creation. Preserve exact-once/uncertain-outcome semantics and verify the implementation before marking complete.
