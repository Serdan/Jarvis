[Deferred][Jarvis 2.0] Durable Challenge suspension on jobs and timers

Parent: jarvis-post-automation-role-queue-evolution-20261122. Explicit Operator requirement: Challenges can block on jobs and timers. Planning only until Automation completion and separate implementation scope GO. Extend existing Challenge semantics rather than introduce a competing blocker system.

Proposed design and acceptance planning

  • Typed durable job condition, referencing the canonical job identity and intended terminal condition; distinguish success, failure, cancellation, unknown and evicted/unavailable evidence. Completion wakes Work for evaluation; it does not imply job success.
  • Typed durable timer condition with absolute deadline and documented clock semantics; deterministic due evaluation after downtime. No model execution remains occupied waiting on a job or timer.
  • Checkpoint and suspend Work atomically with execution disposition, persist dependencies and wake eligibility, release capacity only with actual terminal evidence. Jobs have their own lifecycle and authority; no cancellation merely because an inference execution ended.
  • Evaluate authoritative state when registering and reconciling conditions; event delivery is a prompt for re-evaluation, not sole truth. Prevent lost wake-ups when job finishes before registration or timer already due. Preserve human/dependency challenges, generation and STOP fences, idempotency, indexes, provenance and no duplicate dispatch.
  • Maintain independent state-machine property models: register/finish/cancel races, timer due boundaries, restart, duplicated or reordered events, stale generation, multiple conditions, missing evidence, no premature wake or slot leak. Shrink and replay failures; exercise actual producer/consumer paths with deterministic clocks and fake jobs.

Inventory current job persistence/retention and Challenge wake mechanisms; specify minimal coherent changes and integration with provider-independent Work queues. Exact condition schema, ownership and timing semantics remain design decisions. No implementation, source lease, deployment, scheduler activation or live jobs authorized.
