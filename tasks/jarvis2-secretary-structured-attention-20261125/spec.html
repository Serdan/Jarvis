Secretary role and structured attention

Scope: Deferred Jarvis 2.0 planning/implementation backlog, parent jarvis-post-automation-role-queue-evolution-20261122. No assignment, implementation, deployment or live activation until separate Operator GO after Automation. Inventory and reuse accepted historical Work assets; do not rewrite their completed evidence or implicitly reopen legacy Work/Telos scope.

Direction

Route requests for Operator-owned Challenges through a Secretary executor serving an Operator-attention queue. Secretary may have wide authorized read-only access to Work, specs, evidence, previous decisions and diagnostics, with narrow intervention authority to assess, redirect to suitable roles, consolidate equivalent requests, ask for evidence, or present genuine human decisions. It may not impersonate Operator, grant authority, resolve human-only approvals, modify implementation by default, or fabricate prior decisions. Use ordinary Work, Role, queue, lease, claim and MCP profile mechanisms, not a resident LLM monitor or hard-coded hierarchy.

Deprecate discretionary agent-generated notifications as a coordination primitive. Replace task requests with Work, review requests with review queue, completion with lifecycle transitions, blockers with Challenges, clarification with Work-attached conversations, Operator decisions with Secretary routing, anomalies with structured incidents. Operator-facing notifications remain derived projections of canonical events and decisions, not an independent coordination authority. Preserve historical notification records read-only; do not carry old unread notifications into new projects by default.

Acceptance planning

  • Define durable attention request, routing/merge provenance, exact transition ownership and decision receipt. Preserve original intent, links and dependency semantics through redirection; no loss of genuine human decisions.
  • Distinguish delegated technical resolution from human-only permission or intent; Secretary has no approval authority. Define bounded escalation/reconciliation if Secretary is unavailable, fails repeatedly or encounters genuine urgent issues. Direct urgent escalation and configurability were discussed as proposals, not ratified policy.
  • Prove deduplication, same-decision updates, concurrent routing races, no unauthorized approval, scope separation, restart/replay, and no recursive attention loops. Show pending human decisions conspicuously in the Operator UI and link source Work/evidence. No per-agent notification send endpoint in final scheduled profiles.

Depends on Work-owned authority and queue/Challenge foundations. Coordinate with progression safeguards and manual interpreter access.
