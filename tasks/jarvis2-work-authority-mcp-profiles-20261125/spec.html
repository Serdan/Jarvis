Controlling Operator decisions

Authority belongs to durable Work, independently scoped before execution. A lease grants time-bounded eligibility to claim from a specified queue, not generic authority over Work. Claim binds a valid lease to one eligible Work stage and fencing generation. Roles define instructions and queue subscriptions; MCP profiles expose a limited operation catalog (JarvisWorker, JarvisReviewer, JarvisLead, JarvisSecretary, etc.). Effective authorization intersects authenticated resource/account boundaries, profile operation ceiling, valid lease and claim, and pinned Work authority. Broad observation can coexist with narrow intervention and delegation. No self-expansion of Work authority; independent authorized scoping and explicit revisions. Preserve manual interpreter grants independent of scheduled claims.

Design and acceptance

  • Inventory work-reservation-permissions, work-reservation-credentials, work-uuidv4-explicit-credentials, work-operator-approved-manual-grants and SQLite authority substrate. Historical reservation-derived permissions are evidence, not the controlling new model.
  • Define minimal versioned capability/resource scope for observation, intervention and delegation; specify claim bootstrap scope and authority provenance. Scope expansion must be independently authorized, not implied by a role name or an endpoint.
  • Publish separate server MCP endpoints with profile-specific catalogs and enforce policy on every operation including reads, mutation and external effect admission; wrong plugin/profile cannot confer extra permission. Provider provisioning is adapter-specific. No general-purpose policy language or unverifiable filesystem isolation claims.
  • Atomic yield/completion revokes the execution claim; expiry/reclamation advances fence and rejects stale calls even if an old inference session continues. Duplicate concurrent sessions, renewal/expiry races, stale credentials, cross-queue claims, profile mismatch, read/write separation, self-escalation, and independent review require production-path negative tests. Already initiated external effects retain separate reconciliation/STOP constraints.
  • Demonstrate broad manual interpreter session and Secretary observation, narrow Worker writes, independent Reviewer verdicts and cross-provider Work continuation without authority drift. Exact lease duration, credential mechanism and scope representation remain design work.

Coordinate with provider abstraction and role queues; foundational prerequisite for Secretary routing and progression recovery. No source leases granted now.
