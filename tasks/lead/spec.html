Challenges — corrected source-backed understanding

Lead inspected current source and regression tests. The earlier assumption that no automatic Challenge wait reconciler existed was inaccurate.

  • Client/Modules/ChallengeWorkflow.fs:wakeDependents runs in the same ledger transaction when a Challenge first becomes Satisfied. It walks the reverse wait index, verifies every linked dependency from the same snapshot, and checks exact task/attempt/generation/owner identity. If the owner has no current assignment, is Stopped, and has UnassignedDisposition=NeedsWork, it requeues the blocked attempt and writes the wait, attempt, agent, assignment and lifecycle audit atomically. If the owner is occupied or parked, it persists the wait as Eligible.
  • Client/Modules/ReviewWorkflow.fs:reconcileEligibleOnRelease rechecks every dependency and exact wait/attempt/generation/owner identity on canonical owner-release transitions. It requeues at most one Eligible wait once the owner is free and eligible. The helper is called by completion/release paths and by ChallengeWorkflow.blockAttempt. Parked owners remain Eligible until explicit unpark.
  • ChallengeWorkflow.requeueAttempt is a separate owner-attributed recovery operation: its validated attempt requires attempt.OwnerAgentId = actor. Do not impersonate the blocked owner or call this as a Scheduler.
  • Existing tests cover SatisfiedDependenciesRemainEligibleUntilBusyOwnerReleasesTheirSlot, ParkedOwnerKeepsDurableWakeUntilExplicitUnpark, FinalChallengeResolutionRequeuesEveryDistinctDependentOwner, BlockingAnotherAssignmentWakesPreviouslyEligibleWork, and AlreadySatisfiedDependenciesCannotCreateAnUnwakeableWait. Worker 1 is independently auditing every release call site, actor/audit provenance, idempotency and remaining edge cases.
  • Do not implement a duplicate non-agent Challenge requeue actor. The Worker 1 audit must identify a concrete uncovered release path or a state that remains Eligible while its exact owner is already stopped, unassigned and NeedsWork before Lead considers any additional requeue authority.
