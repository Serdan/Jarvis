Design and acceptance

  • Represent allowed Challenge interactions as explicit configurable directed role relationships in canonical project/role configuration, independent of free-text instructions and agent name prefixes. Model authenticated human Operator as privileged role/principal through the existing trusted human response/admin boundary; no agent may obtain human authority by adopting the role name, self-attaching a role or supplying an actor label. Preserve existing authentication and lease/identity model; policy is authorization layered on it, not a new identity system. Generic relationship check rather than hardcoded Worker/Lead/Operator conditional branches. Keep role configuration management appropriately privileged, versioned and auditable.
  • Default Jarvis escalation policy Worker (and ordinary executor roles) => Lead => Operator; no direct Worker/Reviewer/Adjudicator/etc. human Challenge. Lead investigates and resolves technical issues using existing permissions, structured tasks/dependencies, actual evidence and available routes; only genuinely human decisions escalate. Interpreter may be explicitly authorized for human escalation if Operator chooses; do not infer direct entitlement from its name. Lead owns relevant triage. Distinguish technical job/timer/dependency waits and role-authorized peer condition ownership from human escalation; do not disable valid deterministic dependencies or force needless Lead hops where explicitly configured. Define explicit default/missing/mixed/unknown role behavior, no incidental privilege via aliases or subscriptions. Avoid unnecessary role taxonomy or duplicated authorization system.
  • Enforce shared policy at canonical transactional admission for CreateChallenge, split, assign/reassign, reopen, unassigned dispatch/subscription and every path that can introduce or re-expose a privileged owner. Check actual acting principal, effective owner/role and fresh policy/role membership in same verified snapshot; no bypass via suggested owner, multi-condition ALL, split, role change, legacy ownerKind, cross-project or alternate registered route. Reconcile already-applied operation IDs before rejecting fresh actions; preserve canonical history, CAS, indexes, exact ownership, immutable evidence and existing Challenge wake/requeue semantics.
  • Provide actionable typed refusal identifying permitted escalation route, not a notification. When Lead escalates, link original Challenge/condition/task and concise investigation, why no authorized route exists, exact human decision/options and evidence. Preserve original issuer and wait dependency; no duplicate unrelated challenges or auto-resolution. Lead may resolve its condition from actual human response evidence through existing generation-fenced wake mechanics. Never let a model satisfy an Operator condition as if it were the human.
  • Migration: inspect outstanding existing Operator conditions; preserve legitimate ones and original history, route unauthorized/unnecessary outstanding requests through Lead using explicit disposition rather than erasing or auto-satisfying. Preserve old client and registered-route compatibility/fail-closed behavior; prevent stale configuration writers bypassing policy. Do not create live privileged role or grant any agent new authority until reviewed implementation and explicit appropriate configuration. Existing Operator role/retired identity naming must be reconciled, not reused as an impersonation path.
  • Negative and positive tests on actual registered route and canonical writer: Worker→Lead permitted; Worker→Operator denied with no human-visible event; Lead→Operator permitted under config; unauthorized split/reassign/reopen/unassigned subscription and mixed/unknown roles denied; authorized human response still works; human-only config edits protected; changing configured role relationships works without code edits; old exact retries, concurrent policy edits/CAS, indexes/history, Challenge job/timer waits and requeue fences preserved. Test realistic cross-role flow and concise inbox/prompt output, independent review and incremental deployment qualification. Do not make unrelated robustness work new minimum rollout gates absent demonstrated dependency.
