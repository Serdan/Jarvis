After the awaited authenticated `GetAgentActivity` roundtrip, the scheduler holds `gate` and re-reads the exact schedule, rejecting a mismatched revision, changed record/deadline, disabled/canceled schedule, or wrong status. The new deterministic tests replace both pending and waiting schedules during the verifier's suspended task and assert the old Manager A result cannot launch a new Manager B. No authorization bypass was identified in `HubService.GetAgentActivity`: it obtains user from the authenticated registered session and is observational, not target-agent activity.

The restart test verifies no replacement at 29 minutes, one replacement after cutoff, and no immediate third attempt. The design intentionally permits a replacement after the operator-accepted silent/stale limit; it is not proof the prior browser tab has exited, nor a cross-device leader election for duplicated schedules.
