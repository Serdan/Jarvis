## Blocking P2 finding

See `findings/p2-satisfied-condition-creates-false-blocked-notification.md`.

`Client/Modules/ChallengeWorkflow.fs:365–372` emits a durable **blocked** notice whenever an individual `ResolveChallengeCondition` transaction leaves the overall challenge `Blocked`. Because overall Blocked may be inherited from a *different previously Blocked condition*, simply satisfying a separate Pending condition creates a second false blocked notification. Independent negative regression: Agent 3 issues two conditions, Lead blocks one (one correct notice), Agent 8 satisfies the other (no new blocked condition), yet issuer receives **two notices**. New notification can blame Agent 8 and carry empty reason despite successful evidence. Fix by checking the *target condition's new transition to Blocked*, not aggregate challenge status alone; retain one exact notice per real newly blocked outcome and final satisfied notice. Add permanent negative test and re-submit new frozen SHA.
