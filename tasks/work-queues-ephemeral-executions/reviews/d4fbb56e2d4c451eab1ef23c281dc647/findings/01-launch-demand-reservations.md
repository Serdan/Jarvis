# F01 — P1 — Queue demand is not reserved across Manager passes

**Frozen source:** e737c2da901ae4073f7ecc066781f428020ffdd1, lines 174–196; acceptance 296–297, 301–303, 312.
**Related existing contract:** docs/ManagerHeartbeat.md, authoritative snapshot and durable one-shot launch journal.

**Finding.** The proposed `min(eligibleWorkCount, freeRoleCapacity, freeGlobalCapacity, remainingDispatchBudget)` operates on queue items that remain Available until `ClaimNextWork`. Reserving *role capacity* does not reserve *demand*. With one Available item and five free slots, pass A can reserve/submit one launch. Before its first claim, pass B sees the same Available item and four free slots, and dispatches another. Repeated passes can launch five sessions for one item. The extra sessions may return NoWork, but this is avoidable duplicate dispatch and violates the intended demand-driven bound; a review resolution/requeue race makes it worse. The current Manager journal is local to a client and its process lock does not elect a cross-device leader.

**Required design resolution.** Specify a canonical, project-wide launch reservation state machine and **outstanding demand credits** for each eligible role/queue selection, or an equivalent CAS-based invariant: launches reserved/submitted/uncertain but not terminal must reduce *uncovered eligible demand*, not merely free role capacity. The reservation should not become a persistent task assignment; an aggregate deterministic demand-credit projection or item-generation reservation can preserve claim-after-launch. Specify how alternate queue subscriptions, cancelled or superseded work, no-work exits, and competing Manager devices reconcile demand credits. If single-Manager is a hard invariant rather than shared ledger reservations, explicitly fence competing Manager processes across devices and test it. Local journal evidence may supplement, but must not contradict the sole canonical ledger.

**Required tests.** Two consecutive ticks before any worker claim with 1 item / 5 free slots produce at most one pending dispatch. Two Managers on independent clients racing that tick cannot reserve duplicate demand. Uncertain submit persists and suppresses replacement across restart; positively verified no-work terminal releases its credit. Multiple queue subscriptions cannot count one item twice. Tests 1–2 and 17 currently cover claim uniqueness and empty queues, not this pre-claim launch race.
