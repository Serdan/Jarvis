[P1][Work] Reservation-scoped permissions and blocker boundaries

Parent: work-queues-ephemeral-executions. Hard prerequisite: work-reservation-credentials must be independently Accepted and integrated before implementation proceeds. This task is separate from bearer secret issuance, hash verification and launch identity.

Objective

Make each ephemeral execution's operational authority match its delegated Work rather than its self-declared role or the collection of tools it can technically access. Prevent well-meaning agents from expanding into other projects, performing integration as another role, or solving substantial out-of-scope blockers without authorization. Keep the model small and practical rather than an adversarial sandbox or generalized RBAC framework.

Implementation

  • Associate a versioned and auditable authorization profile with a reservation. Derive it from the pinned Work item, project, purpose, applicable engineering scope and explicit operator/scheduler delegation. Never derive authority from caller-supplied role, agent label, public launch ID, or another reservation's credentials.
  • Start with a few enforceable dimensions: allowed project(s); allowed work unit, source worktree or ref where meaningful; and operation families such as read/inspect, implementation/edit/test/checkpoint, creating/linking prerequisite work, review, integration, scheduling/admin. Permit broader read context when needed. Avoid policy languages, recursive grant trees, speculative privilege taxonomies and duplicate agent principals.
  • On each Work-native mutation authenticate the credential (from the prerequisite task), resolve the reservation, compare its current state/generation and authorize the requested operation server-side. A role label is descriptive; it cannot elevate authority. Missing/expired grants fail closed on new scoped operations. An operator-approved scope change is a visible, audited revision, not a model-selected identity string.
  • Define and implement a proportionate blocker protocol: small directly necessary fixes within delegated scope can proceed autonomously; substantial or outside-scope prerequisites are recorded with evidence and created/proposed as separate linked Work where authorized, then scheduled independently. Do not recursively pursue every newly discovered blocker or undertake unrelated ready backlog merely because capacity exists. Escalate genuinely ambiguous scope expansion under project engineering intent.
  • Denials should identify the denied operation/scope and the allowed path to request a broader reservation or record prerequisite Work. Do not leak secrets. Clearly distinguish mechanically enforced tool boundaries from advisory engineering scope and filesystem/OS protections; do not promise file-level isolation through unrestricted shell commands.
  • Coordinate with existing WQ02 launch/claim ledger, WQ08 scheduler, Work-native tool facade and Task/Role conversations. Preserve existing account authentication and approved repair/debug pathways; scope per-launch operations without making general ledger integrity or operator controls unusable.

Acceptance

  • Worker reservation may edit/test/checkpoint its assigned scope but cannot integrate or schedule unrelated work by renaming itself Lead; separately authorized integration reservation may perform its specific accepted-source integration.
  • Cross-project/unauthorized worktree mutations denied; allowed context reading and blocker creation/linking still function. Substantial blocker is represented as a visible prerequisite rather than silent expansion.
  • Concurrent reservations cannot borrow one another's authority. Revoked/stale launch generation cannot mutate; changes to a grant are explicit, versioned and auditable.
  • Focused .NET 11 Release tests, two-client grant checks and representative blocker cases. Document exact tool coverage and limitations; independent frozen review, accepted source integrated by Lead.

Guardrails: Depend on the completed credential task; no new OS sandbox, server-side durable permission database, general policy engine or parallel Work ledger. Default-off implementation until explicit operator authorization for live scheduling/cutover. No production migration/deployment/restart, hosted GitHub Actions or Codex through Jarvis.
