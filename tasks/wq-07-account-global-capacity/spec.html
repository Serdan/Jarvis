Scope

  • Use authoritative Git-backed account capacity ledger with CAS for reserve/release and idempotent immutable operation receipts; do not duplicate project task/review state.
  • Define two-ledger admission: global token then project demand credit with compensation on definite failure and fail-closed recovery on unknown outcome.
  • Implement verified terminal release and uncertain launch accounting; late/duplicate terminal writers cannot free a second slot or admit an over-limit session.

Acceptance

  • Concurrent reservations from independent clients for final slot have one winner; mixed-role/project executions never exceed cap after restarts.
  • Global grant succeeds but project demand reservation fails/loses response: token either released with proven failure or retained and reconciled, never silently lost/duplicated.
  • No per-role or numbered worker quotas. At global saturation the scheduler-facing read reports unavailable without changing canonical state.
