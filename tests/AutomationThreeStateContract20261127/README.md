The executable is a dependency-free specification model, not a production adapter or proof of actual registered routes. Its literal truth table is the fixture for subsequent implementation; invalid/unknown evidence is not a verified Blocked label, but must never enable dispatch or own-call bypass. ExactSatisfied denotes a still-operative canonical wait before successful requeue, not permission to launch. The canonical Challenge authority must supply this verdict; no parallel store or independent satisfaction algorithm is proposed. Held represents explicit STOP/park; the model conservatively leaves held continuations blocked until authorized requeue. Historical blocked attempts are not the operative wait.

Generated cases cross product wait verdict, active turn, history, dispatch hold, role eligibility, runnable demand and flight. Paired CAS operations exercise both linearizations from identical version/generation; replay and stale-generation checks are deterministic. This models conflicts, not real concurrency or persistence. The production follow-up must bind these cases to verified snapshots, canonical Challenge wake and actual registered ingress/terminal/recovery/scheduler routes; verify legacy ledger compatibility, actual concurrent CAS, attributed activity, STOP authorization, review independence, uncertain-flight reconciliation and TUI/Interpreter display. Neither this fixture nor its success authorizes activation, migration or deployment.
